# Sama

**Sama** is the Somali word for sky.

## Why I built this

We all look up at the same sky.

Whatever continent you were born on, whatever language you pray in, whatever
name your people gave the stars, the sky above you is the same sky above
everyone else. Human beings have looked up at it for guidance for as long as
we have been human. That is not a small thing. It is one of the few genuinely
universal experiences we have left.

I built Sama because I wanted a tool that treats that seriously.

Most astrology apps hand you a verdict and ask you to trust it. You never see
where the numbers came from. You cannot check anything. That has always
bothered me, because the sky is not a secret. Anyone can look up. Anyone can
learn to read what is there. The math has been public for centuries.

So Sama is built the other way around. It computes real planetary positions
from a professional-grade ephemeris, shows you exactly what it found, and only
then offers an interpretation. The calculation and the meaning stay visibly
separate. You can always verify the sky before you decide what to make of it.

## What it does

Sama reads two traditions side by side, Western and Vedic, because they ask
different questions well.

You give it your birth moment and your birth city. It builds your chart in
both systems and gives you a full reading of who you are. From there you can
check the sky day by day, look at the week or month ahead, and ask about the
things people actually go to astrologers for: money, love, health, family,
work, travel, whether now is the moment to make a move.

And because your place on the earth matters, Sama reads that too. Your birth
chart does not change when you travel. What changes is which planets sit on
your horizon. A city that puts Jupiter on your rising line feels different
from one that puts Saturn there. Sama will tell you which is which, for
anywhere in the world, so you can understand not just who you are but where
you stand.

## What I believe about fate

I do not think the stars decide anything for you.

I think they offer a hint. A pattern. A mirror held up at an angle you had not
considered. What you do with that is entirely yours. The point of a tool like
this is not to be told what will happen. It is to understand yourself well
enough, and your moment well enough, that you can choose with your eyes open.

That is what I mean by controlling your destiny. Not prediction. Orientation.
Knowing which way you are facing before you take the next step.

## Why it is free and open

Because the sky is free.

It never made sense to me to lock this behind a subscription. The calculations
are public knowledge. The tradition belongs to everyone. So Sama is open
source, and it always will be. Take it, run it, read the code, improve it,
build something better with it. That is the whole idea.

I am not a technologist by trade. I am an educator, a coach, and a community
organizer who thinks this knowledge should be in more hands, not fewer. If you
know how to make this better, the door is open.

Let's build something powerful together, for us.

## An honest word

Sama is for reflection. Astrology is an interpretive tradition, not a science,
and nothing in this app predicts events. For real decisions about your money,
your health, or your legal life, talk to a qualified professional. The app
will tell you the same thing.

You are always the one making the call. That is the point.
